Lsagna

Lasagna is a baked Italian dish made of stacked layers of wide, flat pasta alternating with fillings like meat ragù, cheese, and tomato or béchamel sauce

  • Meat and Aromatics: 1 lb ground meat (half beef, half pork), ½ cup chopped onion, 1 crushed garlic clove.
  • Sauce: 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes, 1 can (8 oz) tomato sauce, fresh parsley, dried basil, oregano, a pinch of sugar, salt, and pepper.
  • Noodles: 1 package (16 oz) lasagna noodles.
  • Cheese Layer: 1 lb cottage cheese or ricotta, ¾ cup Parmesan cheese, 3 eggs, and 1 package (16 oz) shredded mozzarella
  1. Make the meat sauce: Brown the beef and pork with the onion in a large pan. Add the tomatoes, garlic, herbs, and spices.Simmer on low for 30 minutes.
  2. Boil the noodles: Cook the lasagna noodles in boiling salted water for 8 to 10 minutes until firm yet tender, then drain. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  3. Mix the cheese: Stir together the cottage cheese (or ricotta), Parmesan, eggs, parsley, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  4. Layer the dish: Spread a little meat sauce in a 9x13-inch baking dish. Add a layer of noodles, half the cheese mixture, half the remaining sauce, and half the mozzarella. Repeat the layers once more.
  5. Bake and rest: Cover the dish with foil and bake for 35 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for 10 more minutes until the cheese is golden.
  6. Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
